The ten words

  1. 1. Elōhīm — "God" (the Many Powered)
  2. 2. Yehōvah — "LORD" (the Name behind the title)
  3. 3. Adōnai — "Lord" (the Father's voice)
  4. 4. Ro'i — "my shepherd" (Psalm 23's pairing)
  5. 5. Noach — "Noah" (rest, consolation)
  6. 6. Shem — "Shem" (name, great reputation)
  7. 7. Yerushalayim — "Jerusalem" (foundation of peace)
  8. 8. Yisra'el — "Israel" (he holds onto the heel)
  9. 9. Tziyon — "Zion" (parched place)
  10. 10. Yah — hidden inside "Hallelujah"

A taste, from the guide

"Hallelujah is a Hebrew sentence smuggled into English as a single word: hallelu ('praise') + Yah, the shortened form of the Name. The NMV prints it: 'Praise you the LORD- Yah [Behold The Hand] (Messiah Pre-Incarnate).' The 'Hallelujah' you have sung your whole life had a name in it. Now you can see whose."

Every NMV verse in the guide is quoted word for word from the NMV corpus — the bracketed notes are the NMV's own translation choices, exactly as they print.
